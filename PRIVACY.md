ClearURLs2 continues to protect and respect your privacy.

We do not collect any of your usage data.

Furthermore, ClearURLs2 has no servers and does not embed any kind of analytic hooks in its source code.

Since ClearURLs2, the extension no longer connects to any remote servers.

All tracking rules are now bundled locally within the extension, and are updated only when you install a new version.

Your browsing data never leaves your device!
